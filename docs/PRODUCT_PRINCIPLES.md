# Pocket product principles

These are product constraints, not aspirational marketing language.

## Safety

Pocket must not silently lose or overwrite thoughts. It must keep one clear active owner, and every failed or interrupted operation must preserve the last complete durable state rather than leave a mixture of old and new state.

## Disappearing software

Thoughts are the product. Storage and security machinery stays out of the way, and technical detail appears only when it affects the human's next decision.

## Security

Readable content stays on the unlocked device, and anything leaving the device is encrypted. Security limitations are stated honestly, with no insecure fallback disguised as convenience.

## Lightness

Pocket adds no framework or subsystem without a demonstrated need, no background work without an explicit product decision, and no duplicate owners or data. It loads only what the active mode requires and prefers small replaceable adapters over permanent platform commitment.
