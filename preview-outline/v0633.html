POCKET / A QUIETER OUTLINE

A place to think.

Your thoughts in one calm outline. Click a thought to explore, or click the blank space to its right to write at the end. Escape finishes writing, closes a search, or steps back through visited thoughts. The quiet Copy icon enables one-click copying; click a thought's tiny square for its Unit Sheet, or drag it deliberately to reorder among siblings. Holding over a collapsed sibling head opens its children; the line marks the landing position. For keyboard and touch access, Start writing is also in the More menu.

v0.6.33 native text-selection study. Drag across visible thoughts to select ordinary text and use Ctrl/⌘ C; Copy Mode remains optional for one-click copying. Sibling drop guides and collapsed-head hover expansion are human-tested. Drag does not move a unit into another parent. Experimental Copy-body and alias retrieval remain preview-only, not saved Pocket properties.
